# Demo video script (about 2:45, limit is 3:00)

Use a real lecture PDF from one of your courses (e.g. OS or DBMS slides). Record with OBS or Win + Alt + R.

| Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | A dense lecture PDF, scrolling | "This is a real lecture. Twenty pages of text. I can't study from this, and I'm not the only one." |
| 0:12 | NoteSketch home | "So I built NoteSketch. It turns lectures into notes made of examples, tables and diagrams." |
| 0:20 | Drop the PDF, pick "Quick revision", click Make my notes | "I drop the PDF, choose quick revision, and click." |
| 0:35 | Notes appear, scroll slowly | "Every concept gets one line, then a worked example with real numbers…" |
| 0:50 | Point at a table | "…a timeline table showing exactly what happens step by step…" |
| 1:00 | Point at a diagram | "…and diagrams for anything with a flow." |
| 1:10 | Remember chips, key terms | "Exam traps are highlighted, and key terms are in one place." |
| 1:20 | Answer 2 quiz questions | "Then I test myself with a quick quiz." |
| 1:30 | Type a doubt under a section, e.g. "why can 2PL still deadlock?" | "Stuck on something? I ask a doubt right on the card, and it answers with a tiny example, not another paragraph." |
| 1:50 | Click Flashcards, flip 2 cards, press Got it / Again | "Key terms and quiz questions become flashcards. Cards I miss come back sooner, and progress is saved." |
| 2:05 | Back home, pick Tamil, generate again (or show a Tamil result you made earlier) | "And for students who think in their own language: Tamil, Hindi, Telugu or Malayalam notes, with the English exam terms kept in brackets." |
| 2:20 | Toggle dark mode, show phone view | "Dark mode, works on a phone." |
| 2:28 | Click Markdown, open in VS Code / GitHub preview; click Save as PDF | "I can export to Markdown, diagrams included, or to PDF." |
| 2:38 | Home, "See an example" button | "No server: it runs in the browser with a free Gemini key, and you can try the example without one. Thanks!" |
